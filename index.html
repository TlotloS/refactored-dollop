Our Services

Targeted cleaning for every surface

We restore freshness, improve hygiene and extend the life of your carpets, upholstery and mattresses.

Carpet Cleaning

Removes trapped dirt, dust, allergens, pet hair and odours — improving appearance and extending carpet lifespan for homes, offices and commercial spaces.

Upholstery Cleaning

Couches, dining chairs, office chairs and headboards cleaned with care — lifting stains, body oils, bacteria and odours without over-wetting the fabric.

Mattress Deep Cleaning

Removes dust mites, bacteria, body odours and stains such as urine, blood, ink and sweat — for a cleaner, healthier sleeping surface.

Stain Removal

Targeted treatment for urine, ink, blood, food & drink, mud, sweat marks and pet accidents — matched to the fabric and age of the stain.

Water Extraction

Fast removal of excess water from spills, floods, leaks or over-wet carpets — reducing dampness, preventing odours and mould, and speeding up drying.

Pre- & Post-Occupational

Move-in / move-out deep cleaning for tenants, landlords, estate agents and property managers — carpets, upholstery, mattresses, stains and water extraction.

Please note: Stain removal results may vary depending on the type of stain, fabric/material, age of the stain, and whether previous cleaning chemicals were used. We always apply the most suitable method to achieve the best possible result.
